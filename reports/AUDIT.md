## Demo interpretation

The model targets are synthetic. Recovery percentage can be an observed final field or a constructed strength/ROM proxy, and recovery weeks is observed duration. Neither is a validated clinical outcome instrument. The grouped-CV results and one chronological holdout show the current code path runs and measures those proxy targets; they do not establish patient safety, treatment benefit, external validity, or readiness for clinical use. Missing real-world clinical data, missing adjudicated deterioration labels, optional LightGBM, and the stale twin/XAI analyses are the material open items.

The per-candidate metrics, bootstrap intervals, holdout metrics, and calibration coverage are in `reports/model_benchmark/benchmark.json` and `reports/model_benchmark/weeks/benchmark.json` after running `make all`.
