What is the Dynamic Digital Twin?

The Dynamic Digital Twin is a computational virtual copy of a human patient's physiological and musculoskeletal state. It continuously updates week-by-week using real recorded clinical observations (Pain, Range of Motion, Quad/Hamstring strength, Balance, Walking speed). By combining baseline intake features with machine learning models trained on historical patient cohorts, the twin forecasts individual recovery trajectories and simulates physiological reactions across alternative treatment pathways.
